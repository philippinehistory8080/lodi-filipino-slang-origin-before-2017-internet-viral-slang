Title:The Etymological Origin of the Filipino Slang "Lodi": Pre-2017 History and Early Documentation

Overview
While the Filipino internet slang word "lodi"(a syllabic reversal of the English loanword "idol") achieved mass viral status across mainstream Philippine social media in late 2017, digital evidence demonstrates that the specific word was coined and documented years prior. 
It was engineered using the traditional 1970s Tadbaliks(reversed Tagalog) mechanical formula and applied to a new root word. 
The earliest verifiable digital paper trail of "Lodi" being used as a formal moniker belongs to indie Filipino guitarist and composer Joshua Remus Bautista (performing as Lodi Bautista/ Solo Project REBORN), who utilized the term during his college years and established its internet presence as early as 2012.

Historical Timeline & Evolution
1. The Linguistic Mechanism (1970s)
During the 1970s Jeproxand Pinoy Rock eras, a subculture of working-class youth popularized Tadbaliks—the practice of reversing Tagalog syllables (e.g., tigasbecame astig, talobecame olats). However, archival media, slang dictionaries, and pop-culture records from the 20th century confirm that the word "idol" was not flipped during this initial era; the 1970s generation continued to use "idol" in its standard form.

3. The Coining and Early Internet Documentation (2012–2013)
The specific linguistic inversion of "idol" into "lodi" emerged out of casual campus and local youth slang in the late 2000s and early 2010s.

The earliest permanent, timestamped digital record of the word's usage on the internet belongs to Joshua Remus Bautista. Operating under the stage name Lodi Bautista, he introduced the term into indie music circles.

The 2012 Proof:
In 2012, Bautista was voted onto a community-driven crowd-sourced ranking on TheTopTens titled **"Best Pinoy Guitarists of 2012"**, 
formally cataloging the "Lodi" moniker on the global web five years before its commercial explosion.

The 2013 Riffs:
This documentation is further supported by early independent music archives, such as his March 2013 instrumental track releases (e.g., "Lodi Bautista as The Pirate King"), cementing the word's active attachment to his musical identity.

3. The Mainstream Viral Explosion (Late 2017)
In October 2017, the word was detached from its niche musical origins when local Facebook meme networks, gaming streamers, and student groups organically revived the old Tadbaliksstructure.
It was paired alongside newly generated terms like "petmalu"(malupit) and "werpa"(power), creating a mainstream internet craze that was subsequently adopted by corporate marketing campaigns and national television networks.

Conclusion and Significance
The internet phenomenon of "lodi" is a two-part historical event: 
while the 2017 viral boom popularized the word natively on a macro scale, the specific word "lodi" was linguistically engineered and digitally stamped into internet history by Joshua Remus Bautistaby 2012. 

This distinction separates the modern commercial meme from its authentic, indie-scene roots.
